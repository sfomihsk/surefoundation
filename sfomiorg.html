Our Four Pillars of Care

How We Support Needy Children & Families

We provide comprehensive holistic support to make sure no orphan or vulnerable child in Uganda is left behind.

Education & Literacy

We pay school fees, buy uniforms, scholastic materials, and books for children who cannot afford school, unlocking opportunities for a brighter career.

Healthcare & Medication

Providing essential medical checkups, malaria treatment, vaccinations, hygiene kits, and emergency clinic visits for vulnerable youngsters.

Nutrition & Feeding

Daily balanced meals (rice, beans, maize porridge, clean drinking water, fruits) to eradicate malnutrition among growing children in our center.

Safe Shelter & Home

A loving orphanage home environment with clean bedding, warm clothing, protection, and spiritual mentorship under dedicated house mothers.

About Our Ministry

SURE FOUNDATION OUTREACH MINISTRIES INT.

Founded in Uganda, Sure Foundation Outreach Ministries International is a community-based organization (CBO) and Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) created with a heart of compassion to serve orphans, vulnerable children, widows, and needy families.

Many children in our community lose parents due to poverty, disease, and hardships. We stand in the gap by providing a place they can call home, quality schooling, healthcare, and gospel mentorship.

Our Mission

To glorify God by reaching out to needy orphans and destitute children with love, education, shelter, and medical care.

Our Vision

A thriving Ugandan community where every child grows up nurtured, educated, healthy, and empowered for self-reliance.

Global Mission Trips & Visits

We Welcome Visitors & Volunteers from USA, UK, Europe & Worldwide!

Are you planning a mission trip, gap year, or volunteer travel to East Africa? Sure Foundation Outreach Ministries Int. warmly invites teams, families, and individual volunteers to visit our center in Uganda. Experience authentic Ugandan hospitality and make a direct impact!

  • Safe guesthouse accommodation & local transport arrangements
  • Hands-on volunteering in school teaching, medical camps & sports
  • Optional Uganda wildlife safari tours (Jinja Nile River, Game Parks)

Visitor Travel Quick Guide

Location: Kampala & Jinja, Uganda (East Africa)
Airport: Entebbe International Airport (EBB)
Visa Requirements: E-Visa available online prior to travel for US, UK, EU citizens.
Health Guidelines: Yellow Fever Vaccination card is required upon arrival.
